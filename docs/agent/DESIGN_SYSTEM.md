# Design System

## Visual Direction

Inspired by:

- TailAdmin
- BizSpace
- NexStudio

Do not clone any template.

Target feeling:

- Clean
- Calm
- Modern
- Professional
- Technology-focused
- Trustworthy
- Enterprise-friendly

---

## Colors

Background:

`#F9FAFB`

Surface:

`#FFFFFF`

Primary:

`#465FFF`

Heading:

`#101828`

Body:

`#475467`

Muted:

`#667085`

Border:

`#EAECF0`

Use additional colors only when a real semantic need exists.

---

## Typography

Preferred:

- Geist

Fallback:

- Inter

Typography should be highly readable.

Avoid oversized marketing headlines that dominate the full viewport.

---

## Layout

Main container:

approximately `1200px–1280px`.

Use generous horizontal spacing.

Maintain consistent vertical rhythm between sections.

---

## Border Radius

Cards:

`10px–14px`

Buttons:

`10px–12px`

Avoid excessive pill-shaped UI.

---

## Shadows

Use very subtle shadows.

Prefer subtle borders over heavy shadows.

---

## Cards

Cards should feel similar to TailAdmin:

- white surface
- subtle border
- small radius
- low shadow
- clear information hierarchy

Cards are visual emphasis, not the default layout. Use type, spacing and alignment for supporting information. Avoid nested borders and repeated boxed items. Whitespace is intentional; content hierarchy matters more than component density.

Use eyebrow labels sparingly rather than at every section. Reserve the primary blue accent for CTAs, important links and limited icon emphasis.

---

## Icons

Use Lucide React.

Avoid mixing multiple icon libraries.

---

## Animation

Allowed:

- fade
- small translate
- small hover lift
- subtle image zoom
- button transition

Typical duration:

`150–300ms`

Avoid:

- parallax
- particles
- floating blobs
- cursor effects
- continuous animation
- heavy WebGL

---

## CTA

Primary CTA:

**Zalo contact**

CTA must remain clearly visible but should not dominate every section.

---

## Images

Prefer:

- real project screenshots
- browser mockups
- UI previews

Avoid stock photos where a project/product visual can communicate more clearly.

---

## Content Width

Text paragraphs should not become excessively wide.

Keep reading widths comfortable.

---

## Visual Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- purple neon
- crypto style
- heavy gradients
- strong glassmorphism
- huge rounded cards
- random colors
- excessive shadows
- dashboard-style sidebar
- decorative AI clutter
- fake SaaS metrics
